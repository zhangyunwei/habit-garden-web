## 生成提示词

Create one production game UI sprite: a friendly cartoon tutorial hand with index finger pointing diagonally toward upper left, thumb and three other fingers naturally curled, a short rounded wrist. Match the attached reference's cozy hand-painted farming game style: warm cream peach skin, chunky warm brown outlines, softly painted golden shadows, simple rounded shapes, subtle watercolor/gouache texture, readable at 40 pixels. Reference image is STYLE ONLY, do not draw a lock. Single isolated hand centered, entire silhouette visible, tight framing with 8 percent padding. Genuine transparent PNG background with alpha, no background plate, no text, no arrows, no click rays, no extra objects. Correct hand anatomy, one extended index and four other digits. Output a standalone square asset.
